TitleStrip

The strip on top of a column: 52 pt on the sidebar, 44 pt inside a card (style class card), where 8 pt of canvas above it complete the title bar. It acts as the window's title bar (drag, double-click to zoom) and has a leading and a trailing slot for icon buttons. The article card's strip draws a hairline under itself.

Trailingobject? (null)Content docked right.
PaddingThickness (8,0)Keeps clear of the window's own caption buttons.
BorderThickness / BorderBrushThickness (0) / IBrush? (null)A hairline, e.g. 0,0,0,1 in Line.Separator under the article strip.
Classes card—Height Size.CardStrip.Height (44) instead of Size.TitleBar.Height (52).
Buttons inside the strip take input normally; only empty strip area drags the window.

StatusBar

The thin bar at the foot of a column: status text on the left, a few controls on the right, in 11.5 pt Ink.Secondary. Under the list and the article it draws a hairline above itself; with the style class canvas it sits on the sidebar without one and shows the refresh dot (accent, secondary ink while a refresh runs, Ink.Danger when a feed failed) and "Today at 13.33 · 5 feeds", with the Settings button (an Icon.Settings icon button) on the right.

FilterBar

The list's filter in its title strip: starred · unread · all as three 28 × 22 icon segments in a Bg.Pill track. The active segment is raised (Bg.Panel, BoxShadows.Raised) with an accent icon. Clicking a segment sets Filter; ⌘/Ctrl+1/2/3 do the same from the window.

EmptyState

What an empty pane shows. By default a soft box (300 wide, Bg.Pill, radius 12) with a title, a subtitle and the shortcuts worth knowing under them, for the article card with nothing selected. With the style class compact it is an icon in a round 52 pt tile above a title and subtitle, for an empty list; the classes starred and searching swap the icon for a star or a magnifier.

PanelFrame

An overlay sheet, 400 wide and as tall as its content (at most 600), radius 16, with BoxShadows.Panel, centred over a Scrim that covers the window and catches clicks. The style class narrow makes it 360 wide. Clicking the scrim or pressing Escape raises DismissRequested. Settings show their question in a narrow frame (D30): the question, what happens, then the action in Ink.Danger and Cancel as rows on Bg.Pill.

PanelRow

A clickable row (a Button): title, optional subtitle, and a chevron or a round check. Hover washes the row; a checked row is tinted with Selection.List and its check filled with the accent.

SidebarRow

One sidebar row, 28 pt high. The smart row shows an accent icon for its filter (circle-dot, star or lines); folders show a disclosure chevron on the left, feeds a favicon, sections a 12 pt SemiBold label with a chevron on the right. Counts sit in a badge that is hidden at zero. Selection and hover are drawn by the ListBox.sidebar item, inset 8: the selected row is raised (Selection.Sidebar and BoxShadows.Raised) and its badge takes the accent.

ArticleRow

One article on a rounded tile: favicon · title · time on one line, then "Feed — summary" on at most two lines, and an optional 44 pt thumbnail centred on the right. Unread titles are SemiBold in primary ink; read ones drop to read ink, Regular. The full title is the tooltip.
